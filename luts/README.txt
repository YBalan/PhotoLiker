Colour lookup tables (.cube) for PhotoLiker
===========================================

Twelve looks in the styles people use most. Choose one for the slideshow: set Look to Lut in the settings (6. Slideshow) and click the ... button of the LookFile row;
the file dialog opens in this folder. They are standard 33 x 33 x 33 tables, so any program that reads .cube files (DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, Photoshop, OBS ...) can use them too.

They were made for PhotoLiker by tools/LutGen (a few lines of arithmetic for each look; see the repository) and are MIT licensed like the program: free to use, change and
pass on. They are in the style of the looks they are named after, not copies of anybody's pack. To add your own, put more .cube files here or anywhere else and choose them in LookFile.
